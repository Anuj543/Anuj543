## 👨‍💻 About me

> I help websites get found on **Google** *and* get cited by **AI answer engines** like ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini.

I'm a **Technical SEO & AI Search specialist** from Jaipur. I work on the technical side of search (crawling, indexing, structured data, international targeting) and on making content easy for both search engines and AI models to understand and cite. I also write small Python tools to automate repetitive SEO audits.
